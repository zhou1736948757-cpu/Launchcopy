**Priority: P2, conditional source contradiction; confidence high in the branch behavior, unmeasured likelihood of persistent-suite unavailability.**

The approved appearance contract promises persistent controls and successful persistence before presentation (A:10, 21-28; I:10, 38-40). This finding does **not** assert that normal UserDefaults acknowledgement guarantees a disk flush, that nil occurs on this machine, that N1's restart failed, or that filesystem errors are currently observable through UserDefaults.set.
